A Django framework for building FAIR research data portals with minimal code.

Research teams describe their samples and measurements as Django models. FairDM provides the rest of the portal: projects, datasets and contributors, pages, forms, search, import and export, permissions and an API, following the FAIR principles of findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable data.
